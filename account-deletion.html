Account Deletion Request

App: CHIBBY League Creator

We respect your privacy. If you have an account with CHIBBY League Creator and want it deleted, you can contact our support team using the email address provided below.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to Chibbyball@gmail.com .
  2. Use the subject line: CHIBBY League Creator Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include your Unique Gamer Tag so we can identify your account.
  4. Tell us that you want your account and associated personal data deleted.
Security notice: Never send us your password. We may request reasonable information to verify that you own the account before processing your request.

What happens next?

We will review your request, verify account ownership where necessary, and explain the next steps. We aim to respond within 30 days.

What data may be deleted?

Depending on the information associated with your account, a deletion request may cover your account information, player profile, and associated personal data.

Data retention

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and dispute-resolution purposes. If any information must be retained, we will explain the reason and applicable retention period.

Need help?

Contact us at Chibbyball@gmail.com.
