Privacy Policy

CHIBBYBALL League Creator

Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. Introduction

CHIBBYBALL League Creator helps eFootball players organize leagues, manage fixtures, view standings, and coordinate matches. This policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when you use the app.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, the app may process:

  • Account information, such as your login email and authentication details.
  • Player profile information, such as your gaming name, country, country code, and contact details you choose to provide.
  • League information, including league membership, fixtures, and match results.
  • Match evidence, including screenshots submitted to verify match results.
  • Messages or match coordination information, if you use communication features.
  • Technical information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the app.

The information actually collected depends on the features enabled in the version you use.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage accounts and player profiles.
  • Organize leagues, fixtures, standings, and match results.
  • Verify submitted match evidence and resolve disputes.
  • Help players coordinate matches where communication features are available.
  • Maintain app security, troubleshoot issues, and improve reliability.

4. Storage and Service Providers

The app may use third-party infrastructure providers, including Supabase and Google services, to support authentication, data storage, app distribution, and related functions. Information processed by these providers is subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

5. Sharing and Visibility

Information needed to operate a league, such as gaming names, league membership, standings, and confirmed match results, may be visible to other participants or administrators. Information submitted for match verification may be accessible to people responsible for confirming results.

We do not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed when necessary to operate the service, comply with legal obligations, or protect the rights and security of users and the service.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained for as long as needed to operate the app, maintain league records, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations, or protect the service. You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information by contacting the app developer. Some records may need to be retained where legally permitted or required.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information. However, no internet-based service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

The app is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact the developer so the matter can be reviewed.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the CHIBBYBALL League Creator developer through the official support channel provided for the app.
